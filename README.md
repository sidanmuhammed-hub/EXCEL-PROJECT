Restaurant Sales Data Analysis Dashboard



Dataset Description
This project analyzes restaurant sales data using Microsoft Excel. The dataset contains transaction-level information such as Order ID, Date, Product, Price, Quantity, Purchase Type, Payment Method, Manager, City, Total Sales, Quantity Category, and Month. The data is used to understand sales performance, product demand, customer purchasing patterns, and differences across cities and sales channels.



4. Business Questions
• Which products generate the highest and lowest sales?
• Which cities have the highest total sales?
• Which purchase type and payment method are most commonly used?
• How does product quantity relate to total sales and quantity category?
• What monthly sales trends can be identified from the dataset?
5. Dashboard
The Excel dashboard summarizes key sales information using charts, tables, and visual indicators. It is designed to make product, city, quantity, purchase type, payment method, and monthly sales patterns easier to understand.

6. Key Findings
• Sales performance varies across products, with some products contributing more strongly to total sales.
• The dataset contains both high-quantity and low-quantity products, showing differences in product demand.
• In-store transactions form an important part of the sales data.
• Credit Card and Gift Card are among the payment methods represented in the dataset.
• Sales are distributed across cities including Paris, Madrid, Berlin, and Lisbon.
• Monthly analysis can help identify changes in sales and support inventory and promotion planning.
7. Recommendations
• Promote high-performing products to maintain strong sales.
• Use discounts, combo offers, or targeted promotions for low-performing products.
• Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products to reduce stock-out risk.
• Use city-level sales analysis to target marketing campaigns and promotions.
• Continue supporting convenient card and digital payment options.
• Monitor monthly sales trends to improve inventory and marketing planning.
• Review the dashboard regularly to identify changes in product, city, and sales performance.
